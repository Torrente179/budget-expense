A front door, before the login.

Today a signed‑out visitor hits / and is redirected straight to /login — the product never gets to introduce itself. Four directions for a public page that does, each ending at the same two doors: Create account and Log in.

All four are built from the system the app already uses — coral action and money colour, ink chrome over white sheets, lemon accent, flat surfaces, pill buttons. They differ in anatomy, not paint, so the choice is about structure rather than colour.

Directions

The app’s own surface model used as the page: near‑black hero, one centred coral figure, three phones on a glow, then a white sheet that slides over the fold carrying the five sections as rows. Most on‑brand; the visitor sees the product’s chrome before any marketing.

How to read these

The screenshots are the real anatomy, not captures. Every screen is behind auth, so the phone and desktop frames are the app’s actual structure rebuilt in markup — the Home feed with merchant marks and day separators, remaining‑first trackers, the five Patrimonio buckets in their real accent colours, the magenta Insights bars. They live in screens.js, so all four directions show identical screens and only the page around them differs.

The numbers are sample data. €2,847.30, €48,120.65 and the merchant rows are plausible figures, not anyone’s real ledger. No traction, user counts or testimonials appear anywhere — there is nothing true to put there yet.

Copy is a first pass. It reuses the product’s existing voice and the section definitions from design.md, so nothing here claims a feature the app does not have. Rewrite freely; the structural decision is what these are for.

Nothing is wired. Log in and Create account point at the real /login and /signup routes but these are static files. Shipping the chosen one means a public / route plus a middleware allowance, since the proxy currently redirects every signed‑out request to /login.
