# RANGE fintech mockups

## Summary

Added a third, original mockup system — **RANGE** — under `mockups/range/`. OLED flight-cluster language: available cash as remaining range, GO / HOLD / ABORT on envelopes, a live T+ clock, and an income → buffer → spend energy path. Not derived from `premium-redesign/`, Graphite, or NORTH.

## Product Changes

- Clickable HTML mockups for Home (desktop + phone), Movements, Budget, Net worth, Insights, Review, Import, Wisdom, Settings, and Sign in, plus a gallery at `mockups/range/index.html`.
- Visual language: void black, Saira Condensed HUD labels, Azeret Mono telemetry, heat-red capture, ion-green thrust. The month is a 31-tick trajectory; status is a word.
- Design-only; not wired into the Next.js app.

## Data Model

None.

## Validation

Open `mockups/range/index.html` in a browser and click through every screen. Confirm the range instrument, energy path, and GO / HOLD flags stay consistent.
