# Pavilion fintech mockups

## Summary

Added **Pavilion** under `mockups/pavilion/` after Maison landed too close to the rejected premium stationery language (cream paper, serif figure, letterhead). Pavilion is a split room: deep green stone wall for the headline number, cool daylight for the books. No serif, no cream, no cockpit.

## Product Changes

- Clickable HTML mockups for Home (desktop + phone), Movements, Budget, Net worth, Insights, Review, Import, Wisdom, Settings, and Sign in.
- Horizontal nav on the work side. Status in English (on pace / watch / over). Design-only.
