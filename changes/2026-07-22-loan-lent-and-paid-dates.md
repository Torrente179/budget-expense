# 2026-07-22 — Loan lent and paid dates visible

## Summary

Wealth → Loans now clearly shows the lend date and each repayment date (with amounts), plus last paid / paid-off date.

## Product Changes

- Each loan: **Lent / Prestado** with formatted date.
- Payment history list: date + amount per cobro.
- **Last paid / Último cobro** (or **Paid off / Saldado** when closed).

## Validation

- Open a loan with repayments; dates appear under the person name and in the Cobros list.
