# Up redesign mockup (from-scratch)

## Summary

Added a from-scratch full-app HTML mockup in `mockups/up-redesign/` showing how Budget & Expense would look if redesigned on Up Bank’s product language. New visual system, motion, copy, and sample household. Not derived from `premium-redesign/`, `mockups/up/`, or other local mockup HTML.

## Product Changes

- Gallery: `mockups/up-redesign/index.html`.
- Desktop + phone coverage of live IA: Home, Movements (all/out/in/bills), Budget (pools, new/edit, 6 methods, plan), Net worth (summary and organize buckets + details), Insights (read, calendar, dining), Review, Import, Wisdom (4 tabs), Settings (including category roles), capture keypad, 6-step setup, sign in/up, Go to… and phone account sheet.
- Up-native patterns: spendable-first home, circular slosh pools, black outflows / green inflows, orange stamp CTAs, swipeable section pager (no tab dock, no glass sidebar), day-grouped activity, flick-to-assign review, amount keypad.

## Data Model

None. Static HTML/CSS/JS.

## Validation

Open `mockups/up-redesign/index.html`, then desktop Home and phone Home. Confirm bowls fill and slosh, stamps press in, and sideways swipe moves Home → Movements → Budget → Net worth → Insights.
