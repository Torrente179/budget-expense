# NORTH fintech mockups

## Summary

Added an original high-profile fintech mockup system, **NORTH**, under `mockups/north/`. It is a standalone visual proposal for the whole Budget & Expense product — not derived from `premium-redesign/` or `design-spec.md`.

## Product Changes

- New clickable HTML mockups covering Home (desktop + mobile), Movements, Budget, Net worth, Insights, Review, Import, Wisdom, Settings, and Sign in, plus a gallery at `mockups/north/index.html`.
- Visual language: cool mist canvas, floating studio chrome, dark instrument vault for headline cash, Syne / Outfit / IBM Plex Mono, electric cobalt as the only brand signal.
- Product IA is unchanged (five primary sections + desk destinations). These files are design-only and are not wired into the Next.js app.

## Data Model

None.

## Validation

Open `mockups/north/index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`) and click through every screen. Confirm the rail, vault, and type system stay consistent across pages.
