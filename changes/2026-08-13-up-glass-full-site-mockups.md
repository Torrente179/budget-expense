## Summary

Kept Just Up (spendable, envelope pools, orange Add, black outflows, Plus Jakarta) and added Apple-style frosted glass on chrome, jars, panels, dock, and sheets. Built a complete desktop + phone mockup of every live destination and option so the look can be judged before any product work.

- Gallery: `mockups/up/index.html` (served at http://127.0.0.1:8772/).
- Desktop chrome keeps live IA: Home, Movements, Budget, Net worth, Insights, Review (badge 3), Import, Wisdom, Settings, plus Go to…, EN/ES, EUR, Light, and Add.
- Phone keeps the five-tab glass dock and orange FAB. JP opens the account sheet (Review, Import, Wisdom, Settings, language, currency, appearance, log out).
- Pages cover movements tabs and recurring, budget envelopes/method/plan/edit, wealth summary/assets/debts and every Organize bucket plus details, insights calendar and category, review, Santander/Wise import, Wisdom’s four tabs, full Settings (including category roles and balance reconcile), capture expense/income, six-step setup, sign in/up.
