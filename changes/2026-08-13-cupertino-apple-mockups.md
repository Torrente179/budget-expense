# Cupertino Apple-system mockups

## Summary

New full-site mockup in `mockups/cupertino/`: how Apple would ship this finance app. Mac sidebar + iPhone grouped lists, SF, system materials on chrome only. Not Up, not orange glass.

## Product Changes

- Gallery: `mockups/cupertino/index.html` (http://127.0.0.1:8774/).
- Desktop is a Mac app: translucent sidebar with live IA (Home, Movements, Budget, Net worth, Insights, Review 3, Import, Wisdom, Settings, Log out). Toolbar keeps Search, EN/ES, EUR, appearance, and Add.
- iPhone uses large titles, inset grouped lists, a liquid-glass tab bar, and JP for the account sheet (Review, Import, Wisdom, Settings, language, currency, appearance, log out).
- Remaining envelopes are leftover amounts, not spend bars or liquid jars. Outflows stay black; income and On Pace are system green; Watch is system orange; actions are system blue.
- Same dummy household figures and every live destination from the current app.

## Data Model

None. Static HTML/CSS only.

## Validation

Open http://127.0.0.1:8774/index.html, then Home and the iPhone Home.
