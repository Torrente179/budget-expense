# Maison fintech mockups

## Summary

Added a fourth mockup system — **Maison** — under `mockups/maison/`, in response to RANGE reading as too futuristic. Warm paper, Fraunces for the figure, Source Sans for the book. Written for transatlantic high society (New York, Madrid, Geneva) without costume private-banking or a cockpit.

## Product Changes

- Clickable HTML mockups for Home (desktop + phone), Movements, Budget, Net worth, Insights, Review, Import, Wisdom, Settings, and Sign in, plus a gallery at `mockups/maison/index.html`.
- Language is civil: on pace / watch / over. The month is a quiet ribbon. Available cash sits on a calling-card plate. Design-only; not wired into the Next.js app.

## Data Model

None.

## Validation

Open `mockups/maison/index.html` in a browser and click through every screen.
