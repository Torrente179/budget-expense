# Graphite fintech mockups

## Summary

Replaced the rejected NORTH direction with a second, original mockup system — **Graphite** — under `mockups/graphite/`. Quiet aluminum surfaces, Helvetica, hairlines, and type-as-hero. Not derived from `premium-redesign/` or from NORTH.

## Product Changes

- Clickable HTML mockups for Home (desktop + phone), Movements, Budget, Net worth, Insights, Review, Import, Wisdom, Settings, and Sign in.
- Visual language: flush app chrome (no floating islands), no dark vault, no brand glow. Headline cash sits on the page as Helvetica. Meters are inset grooves. Color is semantic only.
- Design-only; not wired into the Next.js app.

## Data Model

None.

## Validation

Open `mockups/graphite/index.html` in a browser and click through every screen.
